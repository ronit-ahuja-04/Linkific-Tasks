Engineering VESIT AMS: Multi-Tenant Attendance Management with Automated Session Tracking

Academic institutions often struggle with manual roll calls, paper attendance registries, and time-consuming reconciliation. In this case study, I break down the engineering decisions behind VESIT AMS, a production-ready attendance platform designed for college faculty and students.

We addressed proxy attendance through time-gated dynamic session tokens and geolocation boundaries. The backend architecture handles concurrent class check-ins while feeding automated monthly percentage calculations directly into academic reporting dashboards.

Architecture Highlights:

  • Real-Time Session Synchronization: Websocket-driven status pushes for instant faculty oversight.
  • Anti-Proxy Verification: Ephemeral token generation prevents screenshot and link sharing.
  • Report Generation Engine: Automated PDF exports and attendance shortage alerts.

The BMAD Method: Transforming AI Assistance into Structured Agile Engineering

Vibe coding and unstructured prompt iteration often result in architectural debt and fragile codebases. The BMAD Framework (Breakthrough Method for Agile AI-Driven Development) enforces a strict separation of concerns: specification planning, verification gates, and incremental execution.

By treating AI as an agile pair programmer rather than an unchecked autopilot, engineers maintain full code ownership, semantic compliance, and production standards throughout rapid iteration cycles.
